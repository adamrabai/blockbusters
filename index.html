How to play

  1. The team that picks a letter hears the question. First hand up from either team answers.
  2. Every answer starts with the letter on the hexagon.
  3. Correct? The hexagon turns that team’s colour. The team that won it picks the next letter.
  4. Strong wins by joining the red sides. Weak wins by joining the blue sides.
